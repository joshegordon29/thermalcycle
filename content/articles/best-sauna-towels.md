A sauna towel has two jobs: keep the bench cleaner and make the exit easier.

That sounds basic because it is. But the wrong towel makes public sauna more annoying than it needs to be. It gets soaked too fast, takes forever to dry, holds smell, or fills your bag with damp weight.

The best sauna towel depends on whether you need a sitting towel, a drying towel, or one towel that can do both.

## Best Overall: A Midweight Cotton Towel

For most people, a midweight cotton towel is still the most useful choice. It absorbs well, feels good against hot skin, and washes easily.

Avoid very plush bath towels for sauna. They feel nice at home, but they get bulky fast and hold too much water after a public session or cold plunge.

## Best Sitting Towel: Flatweave Cotton

Flatweave cotton is better for sitting because it packs smaller, dries faster, and creates a clean barrier without acting like a bathroom rug.

Look for enough length to cover your seat and the front edge of the bench. That front edge matters when your legs touch the wood.

## Best Travel Towel: Linen Or Cotton-Linen

Linen and cotton-linen blends are good for travel because they fold small and dry quickly. The tradeoff is feel. Linen can be crisp, especially when new.

For a public sauna bag, that tradeoff can be worth it.

## What Size Works Best?

For a sitting towel, choose something around hand-towel to small bath-towel size. For drying, choose a real bath towel or a large flatweave towel.

If you only want to carry one, go larger than you think. A too-small towel is the fastest way to make a good session feel improvised.

## What To Avoid

Avoid microfiber if the towel will touch hot skin often. It packs well, but it can feel synthetic, clingy, and wrong in a room built around wood, water, and heat.

Also avoid towels with heavy decorative bands. They dry unevenly and can feel stiff against the body.

## The Simple Setup

Bring two towels when you can: one to sit on and one to dry with. If you use public sauna often, that small upgrade changes the whole routine.
