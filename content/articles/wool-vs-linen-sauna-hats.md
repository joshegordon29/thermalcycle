Wool and linen both belong near heat. They just solve different problems.

For sauna hats, wool is the stronger insulator. Linen is lighter, easier to fold, and often better for people who want less structure.

## Where Wool Wins

Wool felt is better for hot rooms, longer rounds, high benches, and people whose ears or scalp get sharp quickly. The density of felted wool traps air, and that air slows heat transfer.

If the room is strong and you want to stay longer, wool is the safer choice.

## Where Linen Wins

Linen makes sense for travel, mild sauna sessions, and people who want something lighter on the head. It dries quickly and folds smaller than most felted wool hats.

The tradeoff is protection. Linen can soften the heat, but it will not buffer it the way dense wool felt does.

## Fit Matters More Than The Label

A great wool hat with a bad fit is still a bad sauna hat. The same is true for linen. Look for coverage over the ears, enough room for hair, and a shape that does not squeeze your forehead.

## The Simple Rule

Choose wool for heat. Choose linen for lightness.

If you are buying one sauna hat and you use hot rooms regularly, start with wool.
