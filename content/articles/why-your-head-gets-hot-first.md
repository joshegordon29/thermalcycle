Your head often gets hot first because it is sitting in the strongest part of the room.

Hot air rises. In many saunas, the temperature difference between the lower bench and upper bench is not subtle. Your head, scalp, and ears are high in the room, exposed, and sensitive.

## Ears Notice Fast

Ears have thin skin and a lot of exposed surface. They can feel heat sharply even when the rest of your body is still comfortable.

This is one reason a sauna can feel finished before your body feels fully warm.

## Hair Changes The Feeling

Long hair can hold heat and moisture. Fine hair can feel dry quickly. Everyone experiences this differently, but the pattern is common: the head complains early.

## What Helps

Move lower. Take a break. Add water carefully if the room allows it. Wear a wool sauna hat if you want a buffer.

The goal is not to endure more heat. The goal is to make the heat feel more even.
