A wool sauna hat does not need much care. It needs the right kind of neglect.

Do not over-wash it. Do not machine dry it. Do not leave it balled up wet in the bottom of a bag. Let it dry, keep its shape, and clean it only when it actually needs cleaning.

## Dry It After Every Session

After sauna, shake out moisture and let the hat air dry in an open place. Do not trap it in a wet bag with towels and swimsuits.

If the hat has a shaped crown, set it down in a way that supports the shape rather than crushing it.

## Wash Rarely And Gently

Wool felt can shrink or change shape with heat, agitation, and aggressive washing. If it needs cleaning, use cool water and gentle hand washing.

Do not use a washing machine. Do not tumble dry. Do not wring it like a towel.

## Reshape While Damp

If the hat gets wet enough to soften, reshape it while damp and let it dry naturally. Use your hands. Keep the crown open and the brim or edge where you want it.

## Manage Odor First With Drying

Most odor problems come from storage, not the sauna session itself. Dry the hat fully before storing it. If you carry it home damp, unpack it as soon as possible.

## When To Replace It

Replace a wool sauna hat when it loses structure, thins out, smells despite cleaning, or no longer buffers heat well.
