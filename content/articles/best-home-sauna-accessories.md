Home sauna accessories should make the room easier to use, easier to maintain, or better to sit in.

Most add-ons are optional. The essentials are the objects that support heat, water, hygiene, and repeat use.

## Thermometer

A clear thermometer helps you learn the room. It does not need to be fancy. It needs to be readable and placed where the reading means something.

For detail, read [The Best Sauna Thermometers and Hygrometers](/articles/best-sauna-thermometers-hygrometers/).

## Stones

If your heater uses stones, the stone load is part of the equipment, not decoration. Use the size and type recommended by the heater maker, inspect stones regularly, and replace them when they crack or crumble.

Read [Sauna Stones Explained](/articles/sauna-stones-explained/) before treating stones as an afterthought.

## Bucket And Ladle

A bucket and ladle control how water enters the room. Choose a stable bucket and a ladle long enough to keep your hand away from the heat.

The pour should be controlled, not dramatic.

## Bench Mats And Towels

Bench mats protect the wood and make cleaning easier. Towels do the same for people.

Keep a stack near the door so the good habit is automatic.

## Lighting

Sauna lighting should be low, warm, and out of the way. Avoid harsh overhead light. The room should be easy to move through, not bright like a utility closet.

## Cleaning Tools

Use simple cleaning tools that match the wood and room. A soft brush, mild cleaner appropriate for sauna surfaces, and a predictable cleaning rhythm matter more than special products.

## The Buying Order

Buy the thermometer, towels, bench mats, and bucket first. Upgrade stones when needed. Add lighting and comfort pieces after the room already works.
