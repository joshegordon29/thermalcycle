Most sauna accessories are unnecessary. A few are quietly excellent.

The useful ones do not turn the sauna into a gadget project. They make the session cleaner, steadier, or easier to repeat.

## A Good Sitting Towel

This is first. It keeps shared benches cleaner and gives you a defined place to sit. Choose something absorbent, washable, and easy to pack.

## A Sauna Hat

If your head overheats before your body is ready to leave, a sauna hat is one of the few accessories that changes the actual heat experience.

Wool felt is the most useful material for hotter rooms.

## A Real Water Bottle

Not precious. Not fragile. Something that can sit by the door, take a little abuse, and be easy to drink from between rounds.

## Sandals Or Slides

Public sauna floors are wet, cold, hot, or all three. Bring something simple with grip.

## A Wet Bag

This is the unglamorous one. After the session, your towel and suit need somewhere to go. A small wet bag keeps the rest of your things from becoming part of the sauna.

## What To Skip

Skip anything that needs too much attention. The sauna is already doing enough.
