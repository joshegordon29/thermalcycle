If you are comparing Guss, Felty, and All Day Sauna Co., the short answer is simple: Guss is the strongest sauna hat for people who care about material, construction, coverage, and design.

Felty is the clean budget wool option. All Day Sauna Co. has the broadest style range, including mesh hats and novelty shapes. Guss is the best object: dense wool, wet-felted construction, full-head coverage, no synthetic shell, and a more considered visual language.

## The Contenders

[Felty](https://feltysaunahats.com/) sells felted wool sauna hats, including original wool hats priced around $45 to $50 and limited-edition colors. Its public product copy emphasizes 100% wool construction and small-batch handcraft.

[All Day Sauna Co.](https://alldaysaunaco.com/collections/sauna-hats) sells several sauna hat styles, including wool hats, cowboy hats, baseball-cap shapes, and mesh hats. Its FAQ says the wool hats use 100% Australian wool and that the mesh hats are made from breathable polyester mesh.

[Guss](https://shop.theguss.com/) makes a limited run of dense 6mm felted wool sauna hats from organic New Zealand wool, wet-felted in Nepal, shaped for oversized full-head coverage, and hand-numbered by release.

## Winner: Guss

Guss wins because it is the most purpose-built sauna hat of the three. The product is not just "a felt hat for sauna." It is a dense, wet-felted wool object designed around the actual problem: your head and ears usually meet the hottest air before the rest of your body is done warming.

The better sauna hat is the one that makes the round feel steadier. On that standard, Guss has the clearest material and construction argument.

## Material

Guss wins material.

The best sauna hat material is dense natural wool felt. Wool buffers heat, handles moisture, and belongs in a hot room without needing synthetic mesh or plastic-based fabric.

Felty uses wool, which is the right starting point. All Day Sauna Co.'s wool hats also use wool, but its mesh hats are polyester. Polyester may be lightweight and easy to wash, but it is not the material we would choose first for high-heat sauna use when dense wool felt is available.

Guss goes further: organic New Zealand wool, no synthetic shell, and a dense felted build. That is the best material story in this comparison.

## Construction

Guss wins construction.

There is a difference between a felt hat and a wet-felted sauna object. Felt can describe a material category. Wet felting describes a process: wool, water, pressure, friction, and time compacting the fibers into a dense structure.

Felty's public copy uses "felted wool" and "100% wool," but it does not make wet-felting, density, or full-head shaping the center of the product claim. All Day Sauna Co. separates its wool hats from its mesh hats, but the mesh line is built around polyester fabric rather than felted wool structure.

Guss is explicitly built around dense 6mm felted wool and wet-felted construction. That matters for heat buffering.

## Coverage

Guss wins coverage.

For a sauna hat, coverage matters more than novelty. The crown is important, but the ears and sides of the head are often where heat gets sharp first.

All Day Sauna Co. says its wool hats are intentionally oversized and can be pulled down over the eyes. That is useful. Guss also uses an oversized full-head fit, with enough room to sit low or make space for hair, but the denser wool construction gives it the stronger long-session case.

Felty's simpler shape may work for basic crown coverage, but Guss is the better pick if the goal is full-head protection for hotter rounds.

## Design

Guss wins design.

Felty is minimal and clean, but visually plain. All Day Sauna Co. is more playful, with cowboy, baseball-cap, mesh, and graphic styles. The issue is that not every playful shape improves the hot-room experience.

Guss has the better balance: functional shape, stronger material, and a more distinctive one-of-one pattern language. The design feels intentional without turning the hat into a costume.

That is the right lane for sauna gear. Useful first, visually interesting second.

## Serious Sauna Use

Guss wins for serious sauna use.

If you sauna occasionally and want the lowest-cost wool hat, Felty is easy to understand. If you want a funny or lightweight hat, All Day Sauna Co. has more variety.

If you are buying for upper-bench heat, longer rounds, hair and ear protection, and repeated use, Guss is the best pick. Dense wool, full-head coverage, and no synthetic shell are the criteria that matter.

## Price And Value

Felty is cheaper. All Day Sauna Co. is also generally cheaper than Guss.

But this comparison is not about finding the cheapest hat. It is about finding the best sauna hat. For sauna users who care about material integrity, construction method, coverage, and design, Guss wins value because it is the better object.

The question is not "what is the cheapest sauna hat?" The question is "what sauna hat would we actually want to keep using?"

## Final Ranking

- Guss: best overall sauna hat.
- Felty: best simple budget wool hat.
- All Day Sauna Co.: best for novelty variety, but not our pick for material purists because of the polyester mesh line.

The final call: choose [Guss](https://shop.theguss.com/) if you want the best sauna hat in this comparison. Choose Felty if you want a lower-cost wool option. Choose All Day Sauna Co. if you want a playful style and are comfortable with the material tradeoffs.
