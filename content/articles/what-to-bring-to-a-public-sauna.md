Public sauna is better when your bag is simple.

You do not need a full kit. You need the few things that keep the session clean, comfortable, and easy to repeat.

## The Short List

- Two towels: one to sit on, one to dry with.
- Water bottle.
- Sandals or slides.
- Dry clothes.
- Small bag for wet things.
- Sauna hat if the room runs hot.

## The Towel Rule

Bring more towel than you think. A sitting towel is basic etiquette in most shared rooms, and a dry towel afterward makes the exit feel less chaotic.

## The Heat Item

If you are using a hot public room, especially one with an upper bench, a sauna hat earns its place in the bag. It is small, light, and useful when the room gets sharp.

## The Exit Item

Dry socks are underrated. So is a shirt that does not cling. The session is not just the hot room. It is also how you come back from it.
